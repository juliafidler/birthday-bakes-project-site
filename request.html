For approved staff and program contacts

Request a birthday cake.

We work directly with organizations to coordinate free birthday cakes in a way that is simple, private, and reliable.

Who can submit

Staff members, caseworkers, child advocates, or approved contacts from partner organizations.

When to submit

One to two weeks ahead whenever possible. We will still do our best with shorter notice.

What to include

First name or initials, date needed, age if appropriate, serving size, allergies, and any known preferences.

Unknown details are okay

If flavor, colors, or themes are unknown, leave them blank and the baker can use creative freedom.

Ready to submit a birthday?

Use the request form for individual birthdays, or email us if your organization would prefer a monthly calendar or recurring partnership.
