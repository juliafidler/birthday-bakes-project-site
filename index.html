Our Mission

A birthday cake can be small. Being remembered is everything.

A birthday cake may seem small, but for a child living in foster care, a shelter, or transitional housing, it can be a meaningful reminder that they matter. Cakes & Candles Project exists to make those moments happen with care, consistency, and community support.

Who we serve

Children in foster care, homeless shelters, women's shelters, family housing programs, and residential youth services.

Where we serve

Currently focused on Oakland County, Michigan, with the goal of building strong local partnerships first.

How it works

Birthday cake requests are submitted by staff and coordinated directly for pickup or delivery. Every cake is free, always.

About the Founder

"Something as simple as a birthday cake can mean so much to a child who doesn't always get to celebrate."

Cakes & Candles Project was started by Julia Fidler, a student at Oakland University who has always loved baking and being involved in her community. After volunteering with local nonprofits, she turned the quiet power of one celebration into a promise.

Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Who can request a cake?+

Requests come from staff, caseworkers, or approved program contacts at the shelters and programs we serve — not directly from families — so we can coordinate safely and reliably.

How much does a cake cost the family?+

Nothing. Every cake is entirely free, always. We are funded by volunteers and donations so the celebration never comes with a price.

How far in advance should we request?+

Ideally one to two weeks ahead. That gives our bakers time to plan, shop, and decorate something special — including any allergy-friendly needs.

I want to help but I cannot bake — is that alright?+

Of course. We also need help with supplies, deliveries, outreach, and coordination. Fill out the volunteer form and we'll find the role that fits you.
