About the Founder

A love of baking became a way to help others feel remembered.

Cakes & Candles Project was started by Julia Fidler, a student at Oakland University who has always loved baking and being involved in her community. What began as one idea has grown into a volunteer-powered project serving local programs throughout Oakland County.
